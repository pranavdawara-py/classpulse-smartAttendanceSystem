# Student records and face enrolment

School admins create student accounts, assign editable batches/sub-batches, courses where needed, and optional roll numbers. They set an institution-wide student form: custom fields, requiredness, and whether the school or student completes each field. Student accounts receive a generated-but-editable username and temporary password. Email may be supplied by school or added/verified by the student; verified email is used for password reset so the school cannot impersonate the student after setup.

Enrolment is a guided, quality-checked flow, not merely a profile-photo upload. It must communicate consent/purpose, require at least three suitable single-face images (including spectacles/no-spectacles where relevant), keep images private, generate model-versioned representations, and allow students to add/remove approved images while retaining the required minimum. Each new image must match the approved identity cluster; profile photo is selected only from approved images. School sees all approved images when needed, but normally uses the profile image in lists. Unenrolled students remain available for manual attendance; school and relevant teachers can view missing-enrolment lists.

Out of scope: automatic guardian messaging. Student self-service biometric enrolment is in scope.
