# Student home and attendance history

## Purpose

Give a student/parent a simple private view of their attendance, timetable, profile completion, and face-enrolment readiness.

## Home screen

At the top, an overall attendance card. Below it, one card per assigned subject showing subject name, teacher name, and attendance percentage, with a clear **View history** action. The screen also exposes upcoming timetable entries, profile-completion state, and the face-enrolment task when fewer than three approved photos exist.

## History

Subject history lists dated lecture occurrences, scheduled/original time where changed, final Present/Absent status, and cancellation/reschedule context. The student cannot change attendance.

## Profile and images

Student completes school-assigned fields, verifies/updates email, changes password, and manages face-enrolment photos. The profile photo is chosen only from approved enrolment photos. New photos are quality checked and must match the existing identity cluster; removal is disabled when it would leave fewer than three active photos.
